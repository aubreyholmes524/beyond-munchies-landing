Why this exists

Why does a store run need to be a road trip?

Customers, local stores, and drivers all have a problem that can be solved through a better neighborhood access network.

Customer Problem

  • No ride or limited transportation
  • Kids asleep or family at home
  • Favorite stores too far away

Merchant Problem

  • Lost sales after foot traffic slows
  • Limited delivery infrastructure
  • Hard to reach nearby customers digitally

Driver Problem

  • Not enough local opportunities
  • Too much dead time between orders
  • Long trips that do not always pay enough
One platform. Three problems solved.
